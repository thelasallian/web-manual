---
title: "10.1. Types of Projects"
---

As of writing, the Web section’s WebDev team has released two broad categories of website projects: web specials and microsites. Nonetheless, we are not limited to these two formats.

1. **Web specials.** These are standalone websites developed to cover a particular theme or event, such as an election season or a commemorative month. Although their content is time-bound to the event, these sites are not unpublished afterward. They remain live on the web, but they are no longer updated with new articles or other content.

   A web special can be the **digital counterpart** of a print broadsheet or magazine, adapting its theme and content for an online, interactive format. An example is the Special Elections 2023 [magazine](https://indd.adobe.com/view/51b3e0eb-5605-413d-845d-afd3d47ede7c) and its accompanying [website](https://se2023.thelasallian.com/).

   A web special can also be an **independent project** by the Web section. It might feature new content from a writing section or curate a collection of previously published articles. An example is the [Pride 2025 special](https://pride2025.thelasallian.com/).

2. **Microsites.** These are more permanent, long-term websites. While they have their own unique design, they are built to dynamically display a curated set of articles from the main [thelasallian.com](http://thelasallian.com) website by using the WordPress REST API. An example is the [Rant and Rave microsite](http://rnr.thelasallian.com), which fetches and displays all articles tagged **Rant & Rave** from our main site, sorting them by media type.  
     
3. **Other possibilities.** The Web section is not restricted to the formats mentioned earlier. They are encouraged to conceptualize and propose new types of projects, especially if they have the necessary manpower and skills. Other formats that have been explored by various publications include interactive photo essays, data visualizations, app simulations, and games, among other storytelling formats.
