---
title: "Appendix 3.a. List of Regular Section Tags"
---

| Tag | Description |
| :---- | :---- |
| **University** |  |
| Straight News | It simply summarizes an event that occurred, explaining the major details of what happened. An important rule for Straight News articles is that it is written in the past tense, which means that any reference to the event and any quotes cited must also be in the past tense. |
| News Feature | Unlike a Straight News article, a News Feature is more in-depth, going into greater detail about an event. It is also written in the present tense. |
| National Situationer | These articles look at national issues and explore how these events directly affect the Lasallian community. This usually covers topics beyond the campus, such as new laws and bills being passed, ongoing social issues, and even national elections. |
| For Your Information (DLSU-related) | A For Your Information article, as the name implies, aims to inform the reader about a certain topic, whether it be a process or an office. This usually tackles lesser-known topics that the student body may be unaware of, thus shedding light on them in the process. In some cases, University offices may approach the publication for an article; this is usually the kind of article produced. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| In Review (DLSU-related) | Unlike the previous type, an In Review digs deeper and even questions the process or office. Is the current process fair? Is the office doing its job properly? These are some of the questions the In Review article attempts to answer. Usually, In Review articles are done when a recent incident sheds light on lapses in procedure, e.g., increased cases of theft may be a failing in the security office, student complaints online about inefficient offices may indicate a need to review current processes, etc. |
| Investigative | An Investigative Article is usually made for more sensitive topics that are harder to bring out into the open. |
| 10 Questions | A 10 Questions article is basically an extended question-and-answer portion, but in the form of an article. This is usually reserved for individuals, often in high-ranking positions, who can best discuss certain topics or issues. This can also be a way to better introduce someone newly appointed or elected to a position. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| Commentary | A Commentary, unlike other types of articles, is more opinionated and gives the writer freedom to give their perspectives and personal insights on an issue, which he/she is not allowed to do in a feature. However, like all other articles, it must be research-based and backed by facts. |
| **Menagerie** |  |
| Lounge | Profiles on certain noteworthy individuals. Gives an in-depth look into their lives, passions, and motivations. |
| Plunge | An immersive look at a phenomenon, occupation, hobby, or place. Aside from a topical examination, Plunge articles delve into issues, conflicts, and challenges in the topic covered. |
| Highline | A feature article that deepens the discourse on a relevant news topic by tackling the issue's history, development, and providing a human perspective. |
| Pulse | Pulse dives into the intersection of societal and cultural issues. This encompasses religion, philosophy, politics, economics, gender, etc. |
| 25 Cents' Worth | The section's opinion pieces. They tackle the obscure experiences and thoughts of staffers on a particular topic. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| Rant & Rave | A review of a piece of media, such as movies, TV shows, albums, books, etc. Writers can also delve into social, cultural, and political themes. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. Kindly read through this for a more detailed instruction on the tag. |
| Writer's Recap | Coverage articles. Writer's Recaps summarize events. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| **Sports** |  |
| Halftime Thoughts | Opinion piece on sports-related issues. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| Sports Feature | Default feature. |
| Green Gallery | Feature on an athlete; usually about their journey into their sport. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| Athlete Revisited | Feature on a former athlete. |
| Team Previews | Articles about a team's preparations and expectations heading into their tournament. |
| UAAP + Season # (e.g. UAAP 85) | Articles regarding UAAP games. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| FilOil | Articles regarding FilOil recap games. Usually, the tag is used as the title kicker. |
| **Vanguard** |  |
| Nota Bene | These articles aim to explore on a deeper level and understand the already known concepts or practices in the field (e.g., Provision of Renewable Source Powerplants, Appropriation of Meal Diets, etc.). |
| Versus | These articles highlight topics that divide public opinion or are portrayed in popular culture and analyze the merits of each side vis-à-vis established science (e.g., Why anthropogenic climate change is real, Dinosaurs on film and their real-life counterparts, etc.). |
| Breaking the Surface | These articles are structured to be news-like, as they report on recent innovations and breakthroughs in science, technology, and research (e.g., Third AIDS patient cured of disease, DLSU lands in THE Rankings, etc.). |
| Parallax | These articles investigate complex concepts or advancements in science and technology (S&T) that are often antagonized or misunderstood by exploring varied points of view in a scientific context (e.g., genetically modified crops, the future of nuclear energy, etc.). |
| Behind the Spectacles | These articles highlight the people behind the inventions, discoveries, and achievements that have impacted the field of S&T—regardless of generation (e.g., Derrick Ethelbhert Yu- Outstanding Young Scientist 2012, Lasallian Professors Awarded as National Scientists, Gero Tan Seng Startup, etc.). |
| Behind the Animo | These articles highlight novel or breakthrough research being conducted by students and faculty of the University and scope out the impact that they have on their respective fields. |
| Treatise | These articles provide the Vanguard Section opportunities to write opinionated pieces while also having research-heavy facts injected into the write-ups (e.g., On the Huawei ban). |
