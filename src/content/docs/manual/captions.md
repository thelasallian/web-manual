## 7.1. Event Art

Event Art, also known as Event Highlights, are monthly outputs provided by the A&G section to spotlight significant local and global events. The aim of Event Arts is to commemorate and recognize historical milestones and various advocacies that manage to shape and seek to improve our society, both at home and around the world. The task of the Web staffer for this is to provide the caption for the Event Art.Event Art captions may be two to three paragraphs long, since they won’t be posted on X. There is no strict format, but what is ultimately sought out for are the following:

- Reference to the date through the use of descriptors such as “today” or “this day”;  
- Reference to the event’s frequency, such as “annually” for yearly events or more specific timelines like “every last Monday of August”;  
- The event’s origin;  
- The event’s theme for the year; and  
- At least one source linked below the caption.  
- The overall angle of Event Art captions, apart from the above non-negotiables, may vary as they rely on creative storytelling and proper research. With this, it is up to the Web staffer to set up the direction of the caption. Some options include:  
  - Center the origin and purpose of the event. This is the usual direction. Sample [here](https://www.instagram.com/p/DNImjNbyv-9/?igsh=MWVwZG90YXZ5aWVzeQ==).  
  - Spotlight particular information of the event. This is to raise rarely discussed details or provide variety if possible. Sample [here](https://www.instagram.com/p/DI6Oy-BSLqN/?igsh=ZXVydjQ5MjN5bHdv).  
  - Raising the event’s relevance in current world events. This style provides the longest yet richest caption. Sample [here](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLH5qSOsPr9/?igsh=MW03aWFpb2s2cGo2eA==).

## 7.2. Article

### 7.2.1. Print Article

Print Articles are under the monthly press cycle, which undergo two to four weeks of online circulation on the publication’s FB and X pages. A total of three captions are to be provided for Print Articles: one for FB and two for X.

- Keep the caption within 280 characters for X. Since the link takes 23 characters, the caption itself takes 257 characters. Start with the section kicker (UNIVERSITY, MENAGERIE, SPORTS, VANGUARD, OPINION) and end with the article link.  
- FB has no character limit, and the kicker does not need to be included. Still end with the article link.  
- Before drafting the caption, read the article. The captions for X must be different. A common angle is that the first caption summarizes the article’s overall message, and the second spotlights particular points of the article or discusses its importance. FB captions can be longer, so more points or additional description can be mentioned aside from what is mentioned in the X captions.

### 7.2.3. Press Release

Press Releases are one of the publication’s coverage deliverables for media partnerships, where organizations may promote their upcoming events through **The LaSallian**’s Facebook page, X page, and website. The article and visual will be provided by the partner.  
Captions for Press Releases are similar to that of Online Articles with the 280 character limit limit for X, only with the kicker being PRESS RELEASE. However, the manner of writing is more to describe the event details (date and place) and what it entails (theme and what can be expected in the event).

## 7.3. Sports

The publication participates in various sports coverages, from the UAAP to off-season leagues. Most of the coverages that Web is expected to take care of come from Sports.These captions are also similar to Online Articles, following the 280 character limit for X.   
To ensure complete reporting of game details, sports coverage captions must include the following:

- SPORTS kicker  
- DLSU team and opponent moniker  
- Complete scores, but may be omitted if more important information is included  
- Date and place of match  
- Necessary hashtags (ex: #TLSUAAP87, #UAAPSeason87, #ShakeysSuperLeague)  
  - [Sample 1](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1GKeRKEP4N/). [Sample 2](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1JVeNjbjRp/).  
  - While there appears to be a format among sport coverage captions, showing action through descriptive writing is crucial. This can be done by adding adjectives relevant to the game, rating how La Salle performed, and indicating what stakes the game had, among others. In some cases, usually for intense matches, certain details such as the date and place can be removed to make room for the storytelling. To not create redundancy, try to avoid using the descriptors from the same game’s Game Day, HN, and Buzzer captions.  
    - [Sample 1](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1GQQiiwv9A/). [Sample 2](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1BJoDuv6uZ/).
