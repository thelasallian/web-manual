---
title: "8.4. Backing Out of Coverages (10-22)"
---

Since unprecedented events can occur on the schedule of Web staffers who have volunteered to take on a specific coverage, it is sometimes inevitable that they back out of their chosen coverage. For this matter, a Web staffer is allowed to “10-22” on their previously signed-up coverage. However, they are responsible for finding a replacement staffer who can cover in their place.
