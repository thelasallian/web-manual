## 6.1. Description

The newsroom is the Telegram group chat created to allow for a systematic processing of newsbites. Within this chat, there are six (6) topics: Read First, Univ, Sports, Menage, Vangie, and Pingloi Setup. 

With The LaSallian being DLSU’s official student publication, delivering news as soon as possible is of utmost priority. For this reason, Web’s constant activity in the newsroom is crucial, as this helps the organization disseminate information immediately, especially those that are time-sensitive.

Although there are other news outlets people can rely on, DLSU often has different rules on matters like suspensions, so it is necessary for TLS to promptly disseminate these updates.

### 6.1.1. Topics

***Read First*** consists of everything you need to know regarding the newsroom. Aside from instructions, rules and reminders can also be found here to help guide those who are new to the chat. New policies are also announced here to ensure that everyone involved is made aware.

***Univ*** is where newsbites on University and national news are sent and drafted.

***Sports*** contains the newsbites for sports news relevant to the organization’s target audience. 

***Menage*** includes newsbites on lifestyle and culture and is handled by the Menagerie section.

***Vangie*** holds the newsbites to be drafted by the Vanguard section, particularly those under science and technology.

***Pingloi Setup*** is where group tags are set up and updated by the editors.

### 6.1.2. Process

1. Any staffer is allowed to send newsbites to the newsroom. For those who are not part of the group chat (Intermedia, A&G, and Layout), they may send it to someone who is and confirm with the editor whether this is relevant.   
   2. When the editor gives their go signal, the staffers of the section will be tagged, and whoever is available will draft the newsbite.  
   3. Once they’re done drafting, they will tag their section editor to edit the newsbite.  
   4. After being edited, the section editor will then tag the Executive Editors (execs), composed of the Editor in Chief, Associate Editor, and Managing Editor, for proofing.   
   5. When the exec available has finished proofing, they will tag Web by sending “@merrs” to the chat.

   Anytime around this process, the Web Editor/Web Staffer present may call for other staffers to help with posting by sending “@merrs 10-3” in the group chat. Calling for Web staffers early on will ensure that once the newsbite is ready for posting, a Web staffer is available to post immediately—avoiding further delays.

   

   The role of a Web staffer in the newsroom extends beyond just copying and pasting the final draft to social media. We are expected to proactively participate in the drafting process, which includes retrieving links, gathering visuals, and asking relevant questions to complete tasks efficiently. Additionally, we serve as the final proofreaders for style, grammar, and spelling after the executive editors. Web staffers must proactively engage in the following tasks, among others (some could be done even without waiting to be called with the @merrs tag):

* Retrieving visuals, especially commonly used ones  
* Asking editors what visual to use (preferably early in the process)  
* Sending the visual attribution (via, Photo Credits, Video Credits)  
* Assisting with identifying the news attribution (via), especially if staffers and editors forgot to include them.  
* Assisting with searching for primary sources  
* Shortening links  
* Proofreading for style, grammar, and spelling  
* Proofreading for facts  
* Sharing any helpful information  
* Retrieving links to our previous newsbites, especially for UPDATES, Read mores, etc. (preferably early in the process)
