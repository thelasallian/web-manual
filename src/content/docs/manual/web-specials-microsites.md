---
title: "Web Specials, Microsites, etc."
---

Beyond managing our social media accounts and main website, the Web section—specifically, its web development team—designs and develops websites to showcase content for special issues, commemorations, and themes, among others. These projects are known as web specials and microsites, created in various designs and technologies. All are published under a unique subdomain (e.g., [se2023.thelasallian.com](http://se2023.thelasallian.com))
